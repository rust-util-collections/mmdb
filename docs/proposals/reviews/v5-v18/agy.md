# Joint Engineering Design Review: MMDB v5.0 & VSDB v18.0

## Executive Summary

Auditing the sibling snapshots `./mmdb` (`5.0.0`, commit `f1cb8f6`) and `./vsdb` (`18.0.0`) reveals that **the highest-leverage gaps are not missing RocksDB/Pebble checklist features, but an incomplete cross-repo cutover, two unbudgeted memory-accounting regressions on cutover, a lock-coupling hazard hidden by the initial benchmark protocol, and multi-collection write/durability amplification in real VSDB callers.**

Specifically:
1. **Unfinished v4→v5 Cutover & Broken Migration Path (Pre-Release Blocker):** `./vsdb` `18.0.0` still depends on `mmdb = "4.3.15"` ([vsdb/Cargo.toml](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/Cargo.toml#L19)), wires **none** of MMDB v5's zero-copy/zero-fence APIs ([vsdb/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L378-L633)), and ships an **in-place** `vsdb_migrate` tool ([vsdb/migration/src/universe.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/migration/src/universe.rs#L518-L550)) that immediately fails with `Corruption("missing or unsupported MMDB v5 MANIFEST header")` ([mmdb/src/manifest/version_set.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/manifest/version_set.rs#L128-L134)) once `vsdb` upgrades to `mmdb = "5.0.0"`. Shipping VSDB v18 on MMDB 4.3.15 would force operators through two offline format breaks; shipping on MMDB 5.0.0 without re-architecting `vsdb_migrate` breaks v17 upgrades outright.
2. **Silent RAM Blowup on Cutover (Pre-Release Blocker):** VSDB's memory model ([vsdb/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L1468-L1530)) explicitly relied on MMDB 4.3 ignoring `max_immutable_memtables: 4`. MMDB v5 activates `FlushQueue` up to that limit (jumping peak memtable budget from `2B/8` to `5B/8`), while `BlockCachePool::new` ([mmdb/src/cache/block_cache.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/cache/block_cache.rs#L240-L270)) allocates **two** independent `moka` caches (`inner` + `overflow`), *each* capped at `capacity_bytes` (`2B/8`). Under cgroup limits (`memory.high`), cutting over as-is risks a `>2×` anonymous-memory spike and reclaim stalls.
3. **Material Engine & Collection Bottlenecks Missed by the Initial Benchmark:** Separating write and read phases on `Btrfs (compress=zstd:3)` with constant `0x42` payloads masked (a) `DB::inner` lock contention across `fdatasync`/`write` blocking concurrent `DB::snapshot()` readers in VSDB, (b) unbounded L0 flush SST sizes (`write_memtable_ssts` ignores `target_file_size_base`) paired with single-threaded L0→L1 compaction, and (c) per-transaction B+ tree COW churn and cross-shard non-atomic commits in real callers such as Hotmint ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L161-L203)).

Throughout this report, statements are labeled **`[Measured]`** (backed by repository data), **`[Code Fact]`** (verified directly in source), **`[Hypothesis]`** (causal prediction to test), or **`[Assumption]`** (workload or deployment premise), explicitly separating **engine-level** metrics from **end-to-end VSDB/caller** metrics.

## 1. Key Evidence, Limitations, and Material Issues Missed

### 1.1 Primary Evidence Base
* **Cross-Engine Point-Op Appendix & Raw Runs:** [mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md](../../mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md#mmdb-rocksdb-and-pebble) and [runs.csv](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/runs.csv) compare [MMDB `f1cb8f6`](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/commit/f1cb8f6d06fc039e53bdbee5f55a6971f8ff2c7b), [RocksDB `v11.13.2`](https://github.com/meta/rocksdb/releases/tag/v11.13.2), and [Pebble `v2.1.7`](https://github.com/cockroachdb/pebble/releases/tag/v2.1.7) across 5 shuffled rounds ([mmdb.rs](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/mmdb.rs), [rocksdb.cc](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/rocksdb.cc), [pebble.go](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/pebble.go)).
  * **`[Measured — Engine]`** Single-writer `WalAsync` `Put`: MMDB `276k ops/s` vs. RocksDB `250k ops/s`; warm `get_pinned`: MMDB `1,286k ops/s` vs. RocksDB `1,197k ops/s` and Pebble `802k ops/s`.
  * **`[Measured — Engine]`** 8-writer `Put` scaling: MMDB scales `1.50×` (`414k ops/s` `WalAsync`, `6.08k ops/s` `WalSync`) vs. RocksDB `2.38×` (`594k ops/s` async, `7.30k ops/s` sync; `-30%` async and `-17%` sync gap). Pebble's `1,258k ops/s` `NoSync` is non-comparable on crash boundary because [Pebble `NoSync` acknowledges before `write(2)`](https://github.com/cockroachdb/pebble/blob/v2.1.7/options.go#L350-L367); on comparable `Sync` (`6.42k ops/s`), MMDB is within `5.3%` of Pebble.
* **MMDB Mixed-Workload Profile:** [v5_profile.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/benches/v5_profile.rs#L25-L83) (`2` readers + `2` writers rotating 256 KiB write buffers) and [v5-roadmap.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/docs/proposals/v5-roadmap.md#L1-L120) show **`[Measured — Engine]`** raising `max_immutable_memtables` from `1 → 2` cut write `p999` by `65.7%` (`14.27 ms → 4.89 ms`), whereas `2 → 4` degraded write `p999` (`4.89 ms → 5.32 ms`) while doubling peak queued memtable footprint.
* **Real Caller & Collection Evaluations:** Hotmint integration ([hotmint-v17.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17.md), [hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L145-L315)), ACC migration notes ([acc-migration-primitives.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/acc-migration-primitives.md)), and v18 internal collection audits ([v18-versioned.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/v18-versioned.md), [v18-slotdex-dagmap.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/v18-slotdex-dagmap.md), [v18-trie-vecdex.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/v18-trie-vecdex.md), [shared-mem-pool.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/proposals/shared-mem-pool.md)).

### 1.2 Material Issues Missed by the Initial Single-Host Comparison

1. **`Btrfs (compress=zstd:3)` + Constant `[0x42; 128]` Payload Distortion (`[Code Fact]` / `[Measured Limitation]`):**
   All three benchmark drivers disabled engine SST compression (`CompressionType::None` in [mmdb.rs](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/mmdb.rs)) to isolate storage-engine overhead, yet executed on `Btrfs` mounted with `compress=zstd:3` ([mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md](../../mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md#mmdb-rocksdb-and-pebble)) while writing 128-byte buffers of repeated `0x42` ([mmdb.rs](../../../benchmarks/point-ops/mmdb.rs)). Both 32 KiB WAL blocks and 4 KiB SST blocks compress `>20×` in the filesystem before reaching NVMe. Consequently, `WalAsync` understates OS dirty-page throttling and NVMe write bandwidth, while `WalSync` (`6.08k–7.30k ops/s`) primarily measures Btrfs zstd compression + log-tree metadata `fdatasync`, not realistic physical NVMe write-cache flushes on high-entropy keys/hashes/postcards.
2. **`DB::inner` Mutex Held Across `wal.sync_data()` (`fdatasync`) and `wal.flush()` (`write`), Stalling Snapshot Readers and Background Installs (`[Code Fact]`):**
   The appendix notes ([mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md](../../mmdb-v5-vsdb-v18-improvements.md#mmdb-rocksdb-and-pebble)) that MMDB's write leader inserts into the memtable under `DB::inner`, **but missed a far worse mixed-workload hazard** because the driver separated writes and reads into non-overlapping phases and called `get_pinned` without snapshots:
   * `DB::write_batch_group` locks `self.inner.lock()` at [mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3118) and holds it across **both** `wal.sync_data()` (`fdatasync`) / `wal.flush()` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3186-L3195)) **and** skiplist insertion ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3213-L3228)).
   * Meanwhile, `DB::snapshot_seq` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L2018-L2028)), `DB::get_property` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L2075-L2077)), background flush installation ([mmdb/src/db/flush.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db/flush.rs#L296-L325)), and compaction pick/install ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L1477)) **all acquire `self.inner.lock()`**.
   * **End-to-End VSDB Impact:** Every `MmDB::range` ([vsdb/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L495-L504)) and `MmDB::read_view` (`SlotDex::page`/`count`/`iter`, `VecDex::search` at [mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L535-L542)) calls `db.snapshot()`. Under concurrent `commit_sync()` (`WalSync`) writes on the same shard, **read-only iterators and views stall behind `150–650 µs` `fdatasync` calls**.
3. **Engine Microbenchmark vs. End-to-End VSDB Topology & Allocation Mismatch (`[Code Fact]`):**
   * *Engine benchmark artifact:* `DB::put` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L1860-L1864)) allocates a fresh heap `WriteBatch` `Vec<u8>` on every scalar write (`131,072` heap allocs/frees across 8 threads), inflating allocator contention in `mmdb.rs` relative to batched writes.
   * *End-to-end VSDB topology:* VSDB shards independent maps across `16` `DB` instances (`prefix % 16`), so cross-map concurrency rarely hits 8-thread single-`DB` lock contention; conversely, hot composite collections (`VerMap`, `SlotDex`, `VecDex`, `DagMap`) live on **one 8-byte prefix (one shard)**, serialize mutators externally (`&mut self`), and pay repeated full-value `Vec<u8>` copies (`MmDB::get` at [mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L378-L381)), key-prefix `memmove` (`key.drain(..8)` in `strip_prefix` at [mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L613-L616)), and dummy tombstone WAL fences (`META_KEY_WAL_FENCE` at [mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L478-L493)).
4. **Unbounded L0 Flush File Size Paired with Serial L0→L1 Compaction (`[Code Fact]`):**
   `DB::write_memtable_ssts` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3836-L3882)) splits L0 SSTs **only** at `META_BLOCK_SPLIT_THRESHOLD` (32 MiB metadata $\approx$ 1–2 GiB data), ignoring `target_file_size_base` (64 MiB default). In the 18 MiB benchmark fill, emitting 1 L0 file flattered warm `Get`; in VSDB's recommended 1-shard hot `Namespace` (`write_buffer_size = 256 MiB`, [v18-roadmap.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/proposals/v18-roadmap.md)), each flush emits a 256 MiB L0 SST while `execute_compaction_io` hardcodes `max_subs = 1` for `task.level == 0` ([mmdb/src/compaction/leveled.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/compaction/leveled.rs#L1184-L1189)), leaving VSDB's `max_subcompactions: 4` ([mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L1537)) completely idle during L0 drains.

## 2. Prioritized Roadmap: Pre-Release Compatibility Gates vs. Follow-Ups

Because **both** MMDB v5.0 (`MANIFEST` `DBHeader` v5 + `SST` `TABLE_MAGIC_V2`) and VSDB v18.0 (`VSUNI018`/`VSENG018`/`VSMAPX02`/`VSTYPE04` + slotted `PersistentBTree` pages) already break on-disk compatibility with MMDB v4.3 / VSDB v17, **decisions that affect wire formats, migration topology, memory contracts, or public handle semantics must land before tagging `mmdb 5.0.0` and `vsdb 18.0.0`.** Purely internal engine/concurrency optimizations should follow in `5.1` / `18.1` after validation on uncompressed `ext4`/`XFS`.

| Priority & Gate | Proposal | Boundary | Why Pre-Release vs. Follow-Up |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0 — Pre-Release Gate** | **Unified v4→v5 + v17→v18 Cutover, Memory Contract Fix, & Zero-Copy/Zero-Fence Wiring** | Joint (`mmdb`, `vsdb_core`, `vsdb_migrate`) | Prevents **two** offline migrations for v17 users; fixes `vsdb_migrate` `MANIFEST` v5 crash; prevents `2.5×` cgroup RAM regression; locks `ValueType` wire extensibility. |
| **P1 — Pre-Release API / 18.0 Gate** | **Collection-Layer `VerMap::WorkingTxn` + Co-Located `ShardBatch`** (grounded in Hotmint) | VSDB (`vsdb_core`, `vsdb` strata) + Caller | Fixes cross-shard crash inconsistency in Hotmint `UtxoState`/`VsdbBlockStore` **before** v18 deployments persist random-shard `InstanceId`s (`VSMAPX02`) that would later require data movement to co-locate. |
| **P2 — Post-Release Follow-Up (`mmdb 5.1`)** | **Decouple `DB::inner` from WAL I/O & `SnapshotList` + Two-Stage Write Pipeline + Direct Prefixed Scalar Writes** | MMDB (`src/db.rs`, `src/wal/`) + thin `vsdb_core` call | Internal to MMDB (`DB` wire format unchanged); eliminates reader `snapshot()` stalls under `WalSync` and closes the 8-writer throughput gap to RocksDB. |
| **P3 — Post-Release Follow-Up (`mmdb 5.1`)** | **Split L0 Flushes at `target_file_size_base` + Key-Range Split L0→L1 Subcompaction** | MMDB (`src/db.rs`, `src/compaction/leveled.rs`) | Internal to MMDB (`SST` v2 & `MANIFEST` v5 already support multi-file L0 flush outputs); prevents L0 stalls on large write buffers and sustained overwrites. |

> **Pre-Release Wire Hygiene Note (`mmdb 5.0.0`):** `ValueType::from_u8` ([mmdb/src/types.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/types.rs#L45-L60)) rejects any tag outside `{0, 1, 2}`. Even though engine-level value separation is deferred (§5.1), `DBHeader` in `MANIFEST` ([mmdb/src/manifest/version_set.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/manifest/version_set.rs#L120-L143)) already carries `format_version: u32 = 5`, which cleanly gates any future `ValueType` addition in MMDB v6 without polluting v5.0's wire format. No speculative SST v2 fields should be added now.

## 3. Main Engineering Proposals

### Proposal P0 (Pre-Release Gate): Unified v5/v18 Cutover, Memory Budget Alignment, and Zero-Copy Engine Wiring

* **Operations Changed:**
  1. **Dependency & Offline Migrator (`vsdb/Cargo.toml`, `vsdb_migrate`):** Upgrade workspace `mmdb` from `"4.3.15"` to `"5.0.0"` ([vsdb/Cargo.toml](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/Cargo.toml#L19)); retain `mmdb_v4 = { package = "mmdb", version = "4.3.15" }` **only** inside `vsdb_migrate` (`vsdb/migration/Cargo.toml`) so `vsdb_migrate` reads v17 `mmdb_v4` shards read-only, transforms v17 collection rows/handles in streaming key order, and bulk-writes a staged v18 universe via `mmdb::DB::ingest_sorted` ([mmdb/src/db/ingest.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db/ingest.rs#L126)) before atomic directory swap (detailed in §4.3).
  2. **Memory Budget & Cache Pool Contract:**
     * In `BlockCachePool::new(capacity_bytes)` ([mmdb/src/cache/block_cache.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/cache/block_cache.rs#L240-L270)), stop giving `overflow` a second full `capacity_bytes` budget. Cap `overflow` at `min(capacity_bytes / 8, segment_capacity)` (or unify into a single `SegmentedCache` skipping entries `> capacity_bytes / 8` from cache admission unless pinned by an iterator).
     * In `vsdb_core::common::engine::mmdb::mmdb_open` ([vsdb/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L1494-L1530)), change `max_immutable_memtables` from `4` to **`2`** (matching `v5_profile.rs`'s latency sweet spot and bounding per-shard memtables to `3 × wr_buffer_size` = `3B/8` peak instead of `5B/8`).
  3. **Wire MMDB v5 Primitives in `vsdb_core` & `vsdb` Strata:**
     * Replace `sync_db_wal`'s dummy tombstone write (`delete_with_options(..., &META_KEY_WAL_FENCE)` at [mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L478-L493)) with `db.sync_wal()`.
     * Replace `with_full_key` buffer copies in `MmdbBatch::{insert, remove, delete_range}` ([mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L1280-L1306)) with `WriteBatch::{put_prefixed, delete_prefixed, delete_range_prefixed}` ([mmdb/src/types.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/types.rs#L373-L396)).
     * Replace `MmDB::try_contains_key` ([mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L383-L391)) and `MmdbReadView` existence checks with `DB::contains_key` / `contains_key_with_options` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L1931-L1939)).
     * Expose `MmDB::get_pinned` / `MmdbReadView::get_pinned` (`DB::get_pinned` / `get_pinned_with_options`, [mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L1927-L1948)) through `MapxRaw::with_value` so `MapxOrdRawKey::get` (`ValueEnDe::decode_value(&pin)` at [mapx_ord_rawkey/mod.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/basic/mapx_ord_rawkey/mod.rs#L71-L75)), `SlotDex` (`slot_entry_cnt` & `read_total` at [slotdex/mod.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/slotdex/mod.rs#L207-L238)), `PersistentBTree` leaf/blob reads, and `VecDex` distance scoring decode directly from pinned memtable/cache slices without allocating an intermediate `Vec<u8>`.
     * Rewrite `CheckedBidiIter` / `MmdbIter` ([mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L582-L632)) to call `BidiIterator::step_next` / `step_prev` ([mmdb/src/iterator/bidi_iter.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/iterator/bidi_iter.rs#L77-L163)), allocating `k[PREFIX_SIZE..].to_vec()` directly (eliminating `key.drain(..8)` `memmove`) and adding a lending `for_each_raw` / `MapxOrdRawKey::keys` path that never allocates `Vec<u8>` for skipped values (`MapxOrdRawKey::keys` at [mapx_ord_rawkey/mod.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/basic/mapx_ord_rawkey/mod.rs#L156-L159) currently still allocates and drops every value `Vec<u8>` inside `BidiIterator::next`!).
* **Causal Benefit:**
  * **`[Code Fact — End-to-End]`** Eliminates a guaranteed double offline migration for all VSDB v17 deployments and caps worst-case memtable + block-cache pool footprint from `7B/8` (`5B/8` memtables + `2B/8` dual moka caches) down to `~4.125B/8` (`3B/8` + `1.125B/8`).
  * **`[Hypothesis — End-to-End]`** Under *Assumption A1* (values of `64 B–4 KiB`, warm block cache / memtable), `get_pinned` + `contains_key` + lending `step_next` removes `1` heap `Vec<u8>` alloc+memcpy per point read/deserialize, `1` full value fetch per existence probe (`SlotDex::{insert, remove}`), `1` WAL record + memtable tombstone + sequence number per `sync_wal()`, and `1` `Vec<u8>` value allocation + `memmove` per `keys()` / filtered scan step — cutting VSDB point-get and scan CPU/allocation overhead by `15–35%`.
* **Realistic Alternative:** Ship VSDB 18.0.0 on `mmdb 4.3.15` and defer MMDB v5 to VSDB 19.0.0. *Declined:* VSDB v18 already breaks every collection format (`VSUNI018`, `VSTYPE04`, `VerMap` B+ tree, `SlotDex` 16B total, `DagMap` co-location); forcing operators through a second full-store offline rewrite months later just for `MANIFEST` v5 / `SST` v2 destroys operational goodwill and delays SST v2 memory savings (`PartitionedIndex` vs. v4 resident `Vec<IndexEntry>`).
* **Risks & Correctness Invariants:**
  * **Lifetime & Pinning Invariant:** `PinnedSlice` retains either an `Arc<MemTable>` slab (`MemTablePin`, [mmdb/src/memtable/mod.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/memtable/mod.rs#L59-L99)) or an `Arc<Vec<u8>>` block buffer (`CacheValue`). `MapxRaw::with_value(&self, key, f: FnOnce(&[u8]) -> R)` must **never** leak the `PinnedSlice` into long-lived structs (e.g., `InternalPageCache`), preventing accidental retention of an entire 4–256 MiB `MemTable` slab by a single cached slice.
  * **`sync_wal` Durability Invariant:** `DB::sync_wal()` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3114-L3140)) compares `read.current_sequence()` against `synced_wal_seq` and runs through the write-group leader; any rotated WAL is already flushed to durable SSTs (`fsync` SST + `fsync` dir + `MANIFEST` sync) before `wal_log_number` advances, preserving exact crash-recovery parity with `META_KEY_WAL_FENCE` without sequence consumption.
* **Experiment & Decision Rule:**
  * Run a 2 GiB cgroup-enforced (`memory.high = 2G`) multi-shard write+scan soak (`16` shards, mixed `MapxOrd`, `SlotDex`, `VerMap`) plus Criterion end-to-end `MapxOrdRawKey::{get, contains_key, keys}` and `vsdb_migrate` on a 10 GiB v17 fixture on uncompressed `ext4`/`XFS`.
  * **Decision Rule:** Ship P0 for `5.0.0`/`18.0.0` iff (1) `memory.events:high` stays `0` under sustained 16-shard rotation (`VmHWM <= 1.6 GiB` on `DEFAULT_MEM_BUDGET_MB = 2048`), (2) end-to-end `MapxOrdRawKey::{get, keys}` throughput improves by `>= 15%` with zero `MemTable` pin leaks after flush, and (3) `vsdb_migrate` passes byte-exact logical equivalence and crash-injection at every stage boundary.

### Proposal P1 (Pre-Release API / Collection Layer): `VerMap::WorkingTxn` + Co-Located Multi-Map `ShardBatch`

*(Grounded directly in Hotmint & ACC callers; full caller trace, staged migration, crash/reopen, and rollback analysis in **§4**.)*

* **Operations Changed:**
  1. **`VerMap::working_txn(branch)` (`vsdb/strata/src/versioned/map.rs`, `vsdb/strata/src/basic/persistent_btree/mod.rs`):** Add an RAII/explicit `WorkingTxn<'a, K, V>` over `VerMap` (and `VerMapWithProof::working_txn`) that executes interleaved `get`, `contains_key`, `range`, `insert`, and `remove` against `PersistentBTree`'s in-memory `pending: HashMap<NodeId, Node>` COW overlay **in place** (mutating nodes allocated within the current transaction epoch rather than COW-cloning and tombstoning intermediate intra-block pages on every tx), and flushes `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` pages + `HEADER` + `HEAD` **once** on `WorkingTxn::apply()` or `WorkingTxn::commit()`.
  2. **Co-Located Multi-Map Constructors & `ShardBatch` (`vsdb_core` + `vsdb` strata):**
     * Add `VerMap::new_colocated`, `VerMapWithProof::new_colocated`, `SlotDex::new_colocated`, and `Orphan::new_colocated` (delegating to the existing `MapxRaw::new_colocated`, which assigns `base_shard + k * NUM_SHARDS` so all co-located instances share **one** `DB` shard while keeping distinct 8-byte prefixes).
     * Expose `ShardBatch<'a>` (`Namespace::shard_batch(&self, anchor: InstanceId)` / `vsdb_core::ShardBatch`) wrapping a single `mmdb::WriteBatch` over `engine.shard(&anchor_prefix)`, accepting staged mutations from any collection whose `prefix % shards == anchor_prefix % shards` (returning `Err(VsdbError::CrossShardBatch)` on mismatch) — including `MapxRaw`/`MapxOrd`/`Orphan` puts/deletes, `SlotDex` index mutations, and `VerMap::WorkingTxn::stage_commit(&mut shard_batch)` — and committing via one `commit()` (`WalAsync`) or `commit_sync()` (`WalSync`).
* **Causal Benefit:**
  * **`[Code Fact — Correctness & End-to-End]`** Eliminates **cross-shard crash torn-writes** between `VerMapWithProof` (`utxos`), `SlotDex128` (`addr_index`), and `Orphan` (`total_supply`) in Hotmint `UtxoState`, and replaces `VsdbBlockStore` + `PersistentConsensusState` + `PersistentEvidenceStore`'s `3 × 16`-shard `vsdb::flush()` SST storms ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L307-L309)) with **one** shard `WriteBatch::commit_sync()` (`1` `fdatasync`, `0` forced L0 SST flushes).
  * **`[Hypothesis — End-to-End]`** Under *Assumption A2* (a block of $T = 200$ UTXO transactions = $400$ `VerMap` point edits on a depth-3 `PersistentBTree` of $10^6$ UTXOs), `VerMap::insert` + `VerMap::remove` currently executes `400` MMDB `WriteBatch` commits, encoding and writing `~1,200` slotted B+ tree pages (`~2–4 MiB`) plus `400` `HEADER`/`HEAD` overwrites and `~1,200` intra-block dead-page tombstones. `WorkingTxn` collapses this to **1** batch writing only the final touched subtree (`<= 400` unique leaf/internal pages, `1` `HEADER`/`HEAD` update, `0` intra-block self-tombstones), cutting per-block storage write bytes by `60–80%` and block-execution latency by `>= 2×`.
* **Realistic Alternative:** Tell callers to accumulate edits in a `BTreeMap` and call the existing `VerMap::write_batch` ([vsdb/strata/src/versioned/map.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/versioned/map.rs#L683-L702)), and use application-level WAL recovery across random shards. *Declined:* (1) Real state machines execute transactions sequentially with **read-your-writes** (e.g., tx $j$ spends an output created by tx $i < j$ in the same block, or asserts `get_utxo(&old).is_none()` at [hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L544)); `write_batch` cannot serve interleaved reads/range queries without duplicating a full overlay map + merge iterator in every caller. (2) Reconciling `SlotDex` multi-tier skiplist counts after an async multi-shard crash requires rescanning or custom undo logs in every caller, whereas MMDB already provides atomic multi-prefix `WriteBatch` durability within one shard.
* **Risks & Correctness Invariants:**
  * **`INV-V1` & `INV-B1` (Refcount & Node ID Monotonicity):** In-place mutation inside `WorkingTxn` MUST strictly test `node_id >= epoch_start_next_id` (only nodes allocated during *this* uncommitted `WorkingTxn` may be mutated in place; nodes belonging to `state.dirty_root` at txn start or any committed root are COW-cloned once and recorded in `retired_on_commit`, never mutated). Dropping an uncommitted `WorkingTxn` simply drops `pending` and reverts `next_id` in memory — zero disk writes, zero leaked `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` objects!
  * **`ShardBatch` Two-Phase Memory Publication:** In-memory caches (`PersistentBTree` `InternalPageCache` / `Header` cache, `Orphan` if cached) MUST update **only after** `ShardBatch::commit[_sync]()` returns `Ok(())`; if `write_with_options` returns `Err` (ambiguous WAL state), `VerMap`'s cached generation/root lease must be invalidated so the next access reloads authoritative state from MMDB.
* **Experiment & Decision Rule:**
  * Port Hotmint's `utxo_block_cost` harness ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L600-L646)) to `100k` initial UTXOs and blocks of `T = {1, 32, 256}` txs on `ext4`/`XFS` NVMe, measuring `mutation_us + commit_us`, MMDB `stats.bytes_written`, and `SIGKILL` crash consistency across `utxos`, `addr_index`, and `total_supply`.
  * **Decision Rule:** Ship P1 iff (1) at `T = 256`, `mutation_us + commit_us` drops by `>= 40%` and MMDB `bytes_written` per block drops by `>= 50%` (with `<= 3%` regression at `T = 1`), and (2) `1,000` randomized `SIGKILL` crash-reopen cycles maintain `0` divergence among `utxos`, `addr_index`, and `total_supply`.

### Proposal P2 (Follow-Up `mmdb 5.1`): Decouple `DB::inner` from WAL I/O & `SnapshotList`, Add Two-Stage Write Pipeline, and Add Direct Prefixed Scalar Writes

* **Operations Changed:**
  1. **Lock-Free / Fine-Grained `Snapshot::register` (`mmdb/src/db.rs`):** Replace the `self.inner.lock()` requirement in `DB::snapshot_seq` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L2018-L2028)) with a narrow `snapshot_list` spin/mutex guard that loads `self.read.committed_sequence.load(Acquire)` **under `snapshot_list`'s own lock**, paired with compaction input picking capturing `snapshot_list.as_sorted_vec()` *before* or atomically with reading `versions.current()` (since any SST file in `versions.current()` already has `max_seq <= committed_sequence` at flush/install time). `DB::snapshot()` then **never touches `DB::inner`**.
  2. **Move `wal_writer` Out of `DB::inner` & Implement Two-Stage Group Commit (`mmdb/src/db.rs`):**
     * Move `wal_writer` under write-queue Stage-1 leader ownership (`wal_mu` / leader token), leaving `DB::inner` Strictly for fast metadata transitions (`VersionSet` install, memtable rotation pointer swap, `get_property`).
     * Split `write_batch_group` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3118-L3260)) into a two-stage pipeline modeled on [RocksDB `enable_pipelined_write`](https://github.com/meta/rocksdb/blob/v11.13.2/include/rocksdb/options.h#L1413-L1458):
       * **Stage 1 (WAL Leader, no `DB::inner` lock):** Pop writer group $G_k$, check `FlushQueue` capacity / memtable rotation, reserve `[first_seq ..= last_seq]`, append records to `WalWriter`, execute `wal.flush()` or `wal.sync_data()` (`fdatasync`), and hand $G_k$ to the memtable queue so $G_{k+1}$ immediately begins WAL append/`fdatasync` while $G_k$ inserts into `MemTable`.
       * **Stage 2 (MemTable Leader, no `DB::inner` lock):** Insert $G_k$'s entries into `Arc<MemTable>` (`SkipListMemTable`), publish `committed_sequence.store(last_seq, Release)` (and `synced_wal_seq` if synced) in strict sequence order, and wake $G_k$'s followers. Acquire `DB::inner` **only** for the sub-microsecond pointer swap when freezing a full active `MemTable`!
  3. **Zero-Alloc Prefixed Scalar Writes (`DB::put_prefixed` / `DB::delete_prefixed`):** Add `DB::put_prefixed(&self, prefix, key, value)` and `DB::delete_prefixed(&self, prefix, key)` using an inline stack `WriteRequest` descriptor (`BatchSource::Single { kind, prefix, key, value }`) instead of heap-allocating `WriteBatch::new()` (`Vec<u8>`) in `DB::put`/`DB::delete` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L1860-L1875)), and wire `MmDB::{insert, remove}` ([vsdb/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/core/src/common/engine/mmdb.rs#L393-L411)) to them.
* **Causal Benefit:**
  * **`[Hypothesis — Engine]`** Under *Assumption A3* (8 concurrent writers on `ext4`/`XFS` NVMe), overlapping $G_{k+1}$'s WAL `write`/`fdatasync` with $G_k$'s skiplist insertion plus removing per-`put` `WriteBatch` heap allocation increases 8-writer `WalAsync` throughput by `>= 30%` (closing most of the `414k → 594k ops/s` gap to RocksDB) and 8-writer `WalSync` throughput by `>= 15%`.
  * **`[Hypothesis — End-to-End]`** In mixed VSDB workloads (concurrent `commit_sync()` + `MapxOrd::range` / `SlotDex::page` / `VecDex::search`), removing `fdatasync` and memtable inserts from `DB::inner` eliminates `150–650 µs` lock-convoy stalls on `db.snapshot()` and background flush/compaction installs, cutting read-scan initiation `p99` by `>= 5×`.
* **Realistic Alternative:** Implement full multi-writer lock-free CAS skiplist insertion (`ConcurrentMemTable` as in RocksDB/Pebble) so followers insert into `MemTable` in parallel. *Declined for now (see §5.4):* Under `WalSync` and `WalAsync`, holding `DB::inner` across syscalls and serializing WAL + MemTable of adjacent groups is the primary bottleneck; a single Stage-2 MemTable leader preserves MMDB's uncontended bump `Arena` (`&mut self`) and avoids subtle Miri/Stacked-Borrows CAS hazards while already overlapping Stage 1 I/O with Stage 2 CPU.
* **Risks & Correctness Invariants:**
  * **MVCC Snapshot vs. Compaction Atomicity (`INV-SNAP`):** If thread $R$ calls `snapshot()` and gets `seq = S`, no concurrent compaction may drop versions needed at `S`. Because compaction only reads *flushed SSTs* (`max_seq <= committed_sequence_at_flush <= S`), acquiring `S = committed_sequence.load(Acquire)` and inserting `S` under `snapshot_list`'s mutex **before** compaction snapshots `snapshot_list` guarantees: if compaction picks SSTs flushed after `S` was published, `snapshot_list` already contains `S`; if compaction snapshotted `snapshot_list` first, any SST in its pick had `max_seq <= S` or wasn't flushed yet.
  * **Monotonic Sequence Publication & `fsyncgate`:** Stage 2 must publish `committed_sequence` in strict `first_seq` order (`G_k` before `G_{k+1}`). On WAL `fdatasync` failure in Stage 1 (`fsyncgate`), `set_bg_error` latches fail-stop **before** Stage 2 ever inserts `G_k` into `MemTable` or advances `committed_sequence`, preventing dirty/unrecovered memtable reads.
  * **MemTable Rotation Fence:** When Stage 1 decides to rotate `active_memtable -> immutable`, it must drain the in-flight Stage 2 group $G_{k-1}$ before swapping `active_memtable` and `wal_writer`, ensuring every record in `WAL_old` is applied to `MemTable_old` before `mark_immutable()`.
* **Experiment & Decision Rule:**
  * Re-run the point-ops 1-writer and 8-writer matrix (`WalAsync` and `WalSync`) **on uncompressed `ext4`/`XFS` with pseudo-random uncompressible 128B payloads**, plus a mixed benchmark (`2` `WalSync` batch writers + `4` `DB::snapshot()` range readers).
  * **Decision Rule:** Merge P2 iff (1) mixed-workload `snapshot()` + short-range-scan `p99` under concurrent `WalSync` drops below `50 µs` (vs. `>= 250 µs` baseline), (2) 8-writer `WalAsync` throughput improves by `>= 25%` with `< 3%` regression on 1-writer `Put`, and (3) existing fail-stop/crash-recovery/Miri skiplist suites pass 100%.

### Proposal P3 (Follow-Up `mmdb 5.1`): Cap L0 Flush SST Size at `target_file_size_base` + Key-Range Split L0→L1 Subcompaction

* **Operations Changed:**
  1. **Bounded L0 Flush Splits (`mmdb/src/db.rs`):** In `DB::write_memtable_ssts` ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3836-L3882)), pass `target_file_size_base` (`options.target_file_size_base`, e.g., `64 MiB`) and set `pending_cut = true` when `b.estimated_file_size() >= target_file_size_base || b.projected_meta_size() >= META_BLOCK_SPLIT_THRESHOLD`. Note that `write_memtable_ssts` **already** implements user-key boundary cutting and `carry = b.clip_at(types::user_key(key))` range-tombstone carry across split L0 outputs ([mmdb/src/db.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/db.rs#L3856-L3869)) and atomically installs `Vec<(u64, TableBuildResult)>` into one `VersionEdit`! Importantly, `l0_file_count` / `l0_compaction_trigger` must count **L0 flush runs (or overlap depth)** rather than raw non-overlapping split shards of a single flush, so one 256 MiB flush split into four disjoint 64 MiB L0 SSTs counts as `1` sorted L0 run, not `4` overlapping files!
  2. **Parallel L0→L1 Subcompaction (`mmdb/src/compaction/leveled.rs`):** Remove the `if task.level == 0 { 1 }` hardcode in `LeveledCompaction::execute_compaction_io` ([mmdb/src/compaction/leveled.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/compaction/leveled.rs#L1184-L1189)) when total input bytes exceed `2 * target_file_size_base` and `max_subcompactions > 1`. Derive `split_points` from existing L1 file boundaries (or sampled L0 index partition separator keys when L1 is empty/sparse). Each sub-task opens bounded `TableIterator`s restricted to `[sub_lower, sub_upper)` — reading only the overlapping SST blocks of each L0 file rather than re-reading whole L0 files!
* **Causal Benefit:**
  * **`[Code Fact — Engine]`** The comment at [mmdb/src/compaction/leveled.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/compaction/leveled.rs#L1172-L1174) claiming L0 subcompaction causes "redundant I/O with no benefit" is **false under bounded `TableIterator` range scans**: each sub-task seeks to `sub_lower` via the partitioned index and stops at `sub_upper`, so total SST data blocks read across $K$ sub-tasks equals $1\times$ the L0+L1 data (plus at most $1$ boundary data block per L0 file per split point), while LZ4/Zstd decompression, key-merge, Bloom construction, and block compression parallelize $K$-ways across VSDB's already-configured `max_subcompactions: 4`!
  * **`[Hypothesis — Engine & End-to-End]`** Under *Assumption A4* (sustained overwrites into a 1-shard hot `Namespace` with `256 MiB` write buffer or `4 × 64 MiB` L0 runs on NVMe), splitting L0 flushes at `64 MiB` (tracked as 1 sorted L0 run for both point-read binary search and L0 trigger) + 4-way L0→L1 subcompaction increases L0→L1 drain MB/s by `2.2–3.2×` and cuts L0 write-stall duration by `>= 60%`.
* **Realistic Alternative:** Implement full Pebble-style L0 sublevels with independent partial L0→L0/L0→L1 compactions. *Declined for 5.x:* Sublevels require redesigning `Version`, L0 point-read routing, and `compacting_files` overlap claims; key-range subcompaction + sorted-run L0 flush splitting reuses `build_sub_tasks`, `collect_range_fragments` (`INV-C2a` at [leveled.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/compaction/leveled.rs#L1176-L1204)), and `write_memtable_ssts` `clip_at` with `< 200` LOC of change.
* **Risks & Correctness Invariants:**
  * **Range-Tombstone Split Invariant (`INV-C2a`):** `collect_range_fragments` already gathers range tombstones across all `input_files_level` and `input_files_next` once and clips output SST tombstones at `sub_lower` / `sub_upper` user-key boundaries. Split points MUST remain bare user keys (never splitting two versions of the same user key across sub-tasks).
  * **L0 Run vs. File Count Invariant:** If a 256 MiB flush splits into 4 disjoint 64 MiB L0 SSTs and `l0_compaction_trigger == 4` counted raw files instead of L0 sorted runs (max overlap depth), a single flush would immediately trigger compaction and two flushes would hit `l0_slowdown_trigger (8)`. Scoring L0 backpressure by **L0 sorted-run count** (`max_overlap_depth` or `subrun_id`, exactly as Pebble scores `L0Sublevels`) prevents premature compaction/throttling **and** lets `DB::read` binary-search within a disjoint L0 run instead of probing all 4 split files!
* **Experiment & Decision Rule:**
  * Populate a 10 GiB multi-level DB (128B random values, LZ4 L0/L1, Zstd L2+) and sustain 4-thread uniform + Zipfian overwrites across 40 memtable flushes (`write_buffer_size = 64 MiB` and `256 MiB`) with `max_subcompactions = 4`.
  * **Decision Rule:** Merge P3 iff sustained admitted write throughput improves by `>= 35%` (or L0→L1 wall time drops by `>= 50%`) and foreground write `p99` during L0 drains drops by `>= 40%`, with total compaction write amplification within `<= +5%` of baseline.

## 4. Deep Dive: Collection-Layer Redesign Grounded in Real Callers (Hotmint & ACC)

### 4.1 Real-Caller Evidence in `vsdb/docs/evaluations/`
Inspecting the Hotmint storage integration ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch)) and ACC migration notes ([acc-migration-primitives.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/acc-migration-primitives.md)) exposes three concrete storage-model anti-patterns across the VSDB/application boundary:

1. **Cross-Shard Torn State & Per-Tx COW Amplification in `UtxoState` ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L161-L203), [#L523-L596](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L523-L596)):**
   * `UtxoState` constructs `utxos: VerMapWithProof<[u8; 36], TxOutput, SmtCalc>`, `addr_index: SlotDex128<OutPoint>`, and `total_supply: Orphan<u64>` via `new()`. Each allocates a sequential 64-bit prefix (`P`, `P+1`, `P+2`), landing on **three different MMDB shards (`prefix % 16`) with three independent WALs**.
   * During `apply_tx` across $T$ transactions in a block, every spent input calls `utxos.map_mut().remove(branch, &key)` + `addr_index.remove(slot, &key)`, and every created output calls `utxos.map_mut().insert(branch, &key, &out)` + `addr_index.insert(slot, &key)`.
   * Inside `VerMap::{insert, remove}` ([vsdb/strata/src/versioned/map.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/versioned/map.rs#L642-L650), [#L705-L738](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/versioned/map.rs#L705-L738)), **every single call** reads `Header::get` from MMDB, COW-allocates a fresh root-to-leaf path of slotted B+ tree pages (`[0x10]`), immediately writes an MMDB `WriteBatch` (`WalAsync`) updating `HEADER` (`[0x00]`) + `HEAD` (`[0x01]`) + new `[0x10]` pages, and on the very next tx in the same block emits `[0x06]` dead-page tombstones for the intermediate pages it just wrote microseconds earlier!
   * At block end, `state.commit()` calls `VerMap::commit` (`commit_sync()` on `utxos`'s shard **only**). `addr_index` and `total_supply` remain unsynced (`WalAsync`) on two other shards — **a power loss or kernel panic leaves committed `utxos` divergent from `addr_index` and `total_supply` on reopen!**
2. **16-Shard Forced-SST Flush Storms in `VsdbBlockStore` ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L279-L310)):**
   `VsdbBlockStore` (`put_block`, `put_commit_qc`, `put_tx_index`, `put_block_results`), `PersistentConsensusState` (`8` scalar metadata writes), and `PersistentEvidenceStore` span ~10 independent maps across shards, then call `blocks.flush(); state.flush(); proofs.flush()` — invoking `MmDB::flush()`, which forces **active memtable → L0 SST creation across all 16 shards**, creating tiny L0 SSTs and triggering compaction storms when the caller only needed WAL durability!
3. **Unpinned Point-Read Loop & `u16::MAX` Truncation in `get_balance` ([hotmint.patch](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/evaluations/hotmint-v17/hotmint.patch#L194-L203)):**
   `UtxoState::get_balance` calls `addr_index.page(slot..=slot, u16::MAX, 0, Order::Asc)` (silently truncating balances for addresses with `> 65,535` UTXOs instead of streaming `SlotDex::iter`) and then loops `keys.iter().filter_map(|k| self.utxos.map().get(branch, k))`, reacquiring `with_pinned_branch` (`RootLease` + MMDB `HEAD` lookup) on every UTXO instead of taking one `VerMap::snapshot(branch)`.

### 4.2 Concrete Collection-Layer Design (`VerMap::WorkingTxn` + Co-Located `ShardBatch`)
1. **`VerMap::working_txn(&mut self, branch: BranchId) -> Result<WorkingTxn<'_, K, V>>`** (plus `VerMapWithProof::working_txn`):
   * Captures `base_state: BranchHead`, `base_hdr: Header`, and `epoch_next_id = base_hdr.next_id` once on creation.
   * `txn.get(&key)` / `txn.contains_key(&key)` / `txn.range(bounds)` read directly through `PersistentBTree` rooted at `txn.current_root`, resolving uncommitted pages in `tree.pending: HashMap<NodeId, Node>` first and committed/flushed pages in `InternalPageCache` / MMDB (`get_pinned`) second — giving **zero-overhead read-your-writes** across transactions in a block.
   * `txn.insert(&key, &val)` / `txn.remove(&key)` mutate nodes in `tree.pending` **in place whenever `node_id >= epoch_next_id`**, and COW-clone at most once per pre-existing node (`node_id < epoch_next_id`), staging that pre-existing node ID into `retired_from_base` only if `base_state.dirty_root != base_head_commit_root`.
   * Memory cap: if `tree.pending_bytes > WORK_TXN_SPILL_BYTES` (e.g., `32 MiB` for huge genesis/epoch imports), `txn` spills `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` orphan-safe object rows (`id >= epoch_next_id`) to the shard without advancing `HEAD` or `HEADER` until commit!
   * Finalization: `txn.apply()` publishes the working root (`WalAsync`); `txn.commit()` publishes a new `Commit` (`WalSync`); **`txn.stage_commit(&mut shard_batch)`** stages all `[0x10]`/`[0x12]`/`[0x06]`/`COMMIT`/`HEAD`/`HEADER` mutations directly into a co-located `ShardBatch`!
2. **Co-Located `ShardBatch` & `new_colocated` Across Strata:**
   * Hotmint creates `utxos = VerMapWithProof::new()`, `addr_index = SlotDex128::new_colocated(&utxos, 8, false)?`, and `total_supply = Orphan::new_colocated(&utxos, 0)` (all guaranteed on `shard(utxos.instance_id())`), and similarly co-locates `VsdbBlockStore` + `PersistentConsensusState` + `PersistentEvidenceStore` onto a consensus anchor handle.
   * During block execution, Hotmint opens `let mut sb = ns.shard_batch(utxos.instance_id())?; let mut utxo_txn = utxos.working_txn(branch)?;`, buffers `addr_index.batch_into(&mut sb)` (caching per-slot count deltas in memory during the block and emitting final tier/count/total rows once into `sb`!) and `total_supply.stage_set(&mut sb, supply)`, computes `utxo_txn.merkle_root()?`, calls `utxo_txn.stage_commit(&mut sb)?`, and executes **one `sb.commit_sync()?`**.
   * Similarly, `UtxoState::get_balance` switches to `let snap = self.utxos.map().snapshot(self.main_branch)?; self.addr_index.iter(slot..=slot, Order::Asc).filter_map(|(_, k)| snap.get(&k).ok().flatten())`, fixing the `65,535`-UTXO cap and pinning `branch` once.

### 4.3 Staged Migration, Crash/Reopen, and Rollback Implications

#### A. Staged Offline Migration (`v17` on MMDB v4 $\rightarrow$ `v18` on MMDB v5, plus Optional Shard Co-Location)
Because `mmdb 5.0.0` rejects v4 `MANIFEST`s, `vsdb_migrate` must transition from **in-place mutation** to a **two-directory staged conversion with atomic cutover**:
1. **Stage 0 — Exclusive Lock & Preflight:** Acquire `LOCK` on the source `v17` directory (`src_dir`) and verify `VSUNI017` / `VSENG017` (or legacy unanchored v17). Create `dst_dir = src_dir.with_extension("v18.staging")` on the same filesystem. If a leftover `dst_dir` exists from an aborted prior run while `src_dir` is intact, delete `dst_dir` and restart cleanly.
2. **Stage 1 — Read-Only v4 Scan & Prefix Allocation Plan:** Open `src_dir/shard_XX` via `mmdb_v4::DB::open_read_only_with_options`. Scan allocator ceiling and `__instance_meta__` / caller-supplied handles (`acc-migration-primitives.md`). By default, preserve all 64-bit `map_id`s (`prefix` unchanged). Optionally, for callers opting into co-location during migration (e.g., Hotmint passing a co-location group `[utxos_id, addr_index_id, supply_id]`), allocate co-located v18 `map_id`s on `utxos_id`'s shard for `addr_index` and `total_supply` and return updated handle bytes (`VSTYPE04`).
3. **Stage 2 — Streaming Transform & `mmdb_v5::DB::ingest_sorted`:** Open fresh `dst_dir/shard_XX` with `mmdb 5.0.0`. For each destination shard, stream prefixes in ascending `u64::to_le_bytes()` order:
   * Plain `MapxRaw` / `Mapx` / `MapxOrd`: stream `(prefix || raw_key, raw_val)` directly into `DB::ingest_sorted`.
   * `SlotDex`: emit `[0x00]`/`[0x01]` rows unchanged and transform `[0x02]` (`TOTAL_KEY`) from 8B to 16B `(total, generation = 0)` ([vsdb/migration/src/slotdex.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/migration/src/slotdex.rs#L127-L143)) directly into the sorted stream.
   * `VerMap`: run the existing reachable-commit B+ tree builder ([vsdb/migration/src/versioned.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/migration/src/versioned.rs#L136-L165)), emitting `[0x00]` `Header(v18)`, `[0x01..0x04]` metadata, `[0x07]` clean reference checkpoint, and `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` slotted pages/blobs in sorted key order into `DB::ingest_sorted` (`O(1)` bottommost L6 SST creation, zero WAL/compaction write amplification!).
   * `DagMap`: emit remapped parent/child rows onto the single co-located prefix.
4. **Stage 3 — Durable Sync & Atomic Directory Cutover:** Write `VSUNI018`, `VSENG018`, `__prefix_alloc__`, and converted `__instance_meta__` inside `dst_dir/__SYSTEM__`, `fsync` all files and shard/parent directories, then cut over via Linux `renameat2(RENAME_EXCHANGE)` between `src_dir` and `dst_dir` (or durable 3-step rename `src_dir -> src_dir.v17.bak`, `dst_dir -> src_dir` + `fsync(parent)`), keeping `src_dir.v17.bak` until operator verification completes.

#### B. Crash / Reopen Implications
* **Crash During Migration:** Before `renameat2` / final rename, `src_dir` is 100% untouched read-only MMDB v4 / VSDB v17; reopening with v17 works immediately, and re-running `vsdb_migrate` wipes `v18.staging` and succeeds idempotently. After atomic rename, `src_dir` is a fully `fsync`ed v18 / MMDB v5 universe with clean `[0x07]` `VerMap` reference checkpoints (so first v18 reopen skips full graph recount).
* **Crash During Runtime Block Execution (`WorkingTxn` + `ShardBatch`):**
  * *Crash before `sb.commit_sync()` (or `txn.apply()`):* Neither `VerMap` `HEAD`/`HEADER` nor `SlotDex` nor `Orphan` has written to MMDB (even if `WorkingTxn` spilled `>32 MiB` of `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` nodes, those IDs are `>= persisted Header.next_id`, and `Header` was not updated). On reopen, MMDB replays WAL up to the previous committed block; `VerMap::open` (`verify_and_gc` in [vsdb/strata/src/versioned/repair.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/versioned/repair.rs)) detects no dirty mismatch if `[0x07]` was clean, or sweeps any unreferenced spilled `[0x10]`/`[0x12]` pages if dirty, and `utxos`, `addr_index`, and `total_supply` match the exact prior block height!
  * *Crash during/after `sb.commit_sync()`:* Because all co-located prefixes reside in **one** MMDB `WriteBatch` record in **one** shard WAL with CRC32 validation, WAL recovery is strictly **all-or-nothing** across `utxos`, `addr_index`, and `total_supply`. Disposable `VerMapWithProof` trie checkpoints (`save_cache`, [vsdb/strata/src/trie/proof.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/trie/proof.rs#L160-L178)) validate `(universe, map_id, commit, tree_root)` on reopen and incrementally catch up via `TreeDiff` if behind the latest `CommitId`.

#### C. Rollback Implications
* **Binary/Deployment Rollback (v18 $\rightarrow$ v17):** Retaining `src_dir.v17.bak` after Stage 3 gives instant `O(1)` rollback prior to accepting new v18 traffic. Once new v18 blocks are committed, rollback requires either replaying blocks from network/consensus log onto `src_dir.v17.bak` or running a logical v18-export tool (MMDB v5 SSTs cannot be opened by MMDB v4.3).
* **Runtime Transaction / Block Abort Rollback:**
  * *Intra-block abort (invalid tx or consensus proposal timeout before `commit_sync`):* Dropping `WorkingTxn` and `ShardBatch` rolls back all in-memory B+ tree pages, `SlotDex` deltas, and `Orphan` edits in **`O(1)` disk I/O** (`0` tombstones written, `VerMapWithProof::working_trie` untouched until `merkle_root` or reset from `committed_trie`).
  * *Post-`apply()` working rollback (`VerMap::discard(branch)`):* If a caller explicitly published working state via `txn.apply()` and later aborts before `commit()`, `VerMap::discard(branch)` ([vsdb/strata/src/versioned/map.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/versioned/map.rs#L789)) resets `dirty_root` to `head.root`; co-located auxiliary indexes (`SlotDex`) should therefore only be committed inside the block-final `ShardBatch` alongside `utxo_txn.stage_commit(&mut sb)`, never half-way through speculative execution.

## 5. Ideas Deferred or Rejected and Why

1. **Reject for MMDB v5.0: Engine-Level Value Separation (BlobDB / Titan-Style Blob Files)**
   * *Why:* In VSDB, the primary large-value mutating structure (`VerMap` / `PersistentBTree`) **already implements collection-layer value separation** (`INLINE_VALUE_LIMIT = 1024`, storing payloads `> 1 KiB` as immutable `[0x12]` blob entries referenced by 8-byte `NodeId` descriptors in `[0x10]` leaf pages without rewriting blobs during B+ tree splits/merges; [vsdb/strata/src/basic/persistent_btree/nodes.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/basic/persistent_btree/nodes.rs#L18-L35)). Adding a second engine-level blob layer in MMDB v5.0 would introduce blob garbage-collection write amplification, foreground/GC race conditions with `PinnedSlice` and `Snapshot`, disable range-scan locality, and break SST/WAL `ValueType` wire formats ([mmdb/src/types.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/types.rs#L45-L60)) without measured evidence that non-`VerMap` collections overwrite `>= 16 KiB` values heavily. Re-evaluate only for MMDB v6 if standalone non-`VerMap` large-value churn emerges.
2. **Reject: RocksDB-Style Column Families and Engine-Level Optimistic/Pessimistic Transactions (`GetForUpdate`)**
   * *Why:* VSDB already achieves multi-tenancy and per-workload isolation through 8-byte key prefixes, `MapxRaw::new_colocated` (atomic single-WAL `ShardBatch`), and `Namespace` (separate `EngineSizing` / 1-shard hot isolation). General multi-writer `GetForUpdate` conflict tracking inside MMDB duplicates VSDB's `&mut self` / single-writer state-machine discipline (Hotmint/ACC serialize block execution at the state machine) while taxing every batch commit.
3. **Reject: Associative `Merge` Operators and Pebble-Style `RangeKeySet` Versioned Spans**
   * *Why:* Neither VSDB collection semantics nor Hotmint/ACC can safely use blind associative merge operands: `SlotDex::insert`/`remove` must check key existence (`TAG_ENTRY`) before incrementing/decrementing tier counters (`TAG_LEVEL`) and checks an 8-byte `generation` epoch (`TOTAL_KEY`), while `VerMap` maintains reference-counted COW pages. Blind `Merge` operands also degrade point reads across L0 runs. Similarly, VSDB uses `delete_range_prefixed` only for prefix clear (`MapxRaw::clear`), needing zero `RangeKeySet` machinery.
4. **Defer (`18.x` / `5.x`): Process-Wide `BlockCachePool` (Tier iii) and Cross-Engine `WriteBufferManager`**
   * *Why:* As VSDB's own analysis notes ([shared-mem-pool.md](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/docs/proposals/shared-mem-pool.md)), sharing one `BlockCachePool` across independent `Namespace`s introduces cross-tenant cache eviction and violates per-namespace `VsdbOptions::with_mem_budget_mb` predictability, while cross-DB `WriteBufferManager` throttling risks cross-shard flush deadlocks unless flush threads are globally scheduled. Fixing `BlockCachePool` `overflow` double-budgeting and setting `max_immutable_memtables = 2` (Proposal P0) solves the immediate RAM blowup cleanly with zero cross-namespace coupling.
5. **Defer (`5.x`): Multi-Writer Lock-Free CAS Skiplist Splicing in `SkipListMemTable`**
   * *Why:* `ConcurrentSkipList` ([mmdb/src/memtable/skiplist_impl.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/memtable/skiplist_impl.rs#L1-L120)) currently relies on single-writer `&mut Arena` bump allocation (`O(1)`, zero atomic CAS retries, clean Miri provenance). Until Stage-1 WAL I/O is decoupled from Stage-2 MemTable insertion (Proposal P2), parallelizing skiplist splicing adds high `unsafe` memory-ordering risk for marginal gain, especially in VSDB where independent maps already fan out across 16 shards.
6. **Defer (`5.x`): Backward Skiplist Pointers in `MemTable` and `io_uring` Batched `MultiGet`**
   * *Why:* Reverse memtable stepping (`find_less_than` in [mmdb/src/memtable/skiplist.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/mmdb/blob/4916abbcb6222aa225efb3d95be66f71ee5a2ad6/src/memtable/skiplist.rs)) only affects un-flushed active/immutable memtable rows, and VSDB v18 already eliminated pathological reverse-tombstone scans in `SlotDex` via bounded forward chunk stepping ([vsdb/strata/src/slotdex/query.rs](https://github.com/rust-util-collections/vsdb/blob/7082a888e8defc9a563653b14b0e0b16277f6496/strata/src/slotdex/query.rs)). Adding a `prev` pointer per skiplist node permanently inflates node header RAM. Likewise, `io_uring` `MultiGet` only helps cold-NVMe high-QD scatter reads where working set $\gg$ RAM; `VerMap::snapshot` + `get_pinned` (P0 + P1) should land and be profiled on larger-than-RAM state first.
